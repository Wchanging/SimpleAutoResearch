# Single-Source Review Criteria

## Review Goal

Check that a review of at most one citable source is accurate, useful, and
explicit about the difference between a source claim and observed evidence.

## Required Checks

- Source-specific claims need nearby citations from the current run.
- Do not require a method taxonomy or cross-paper comparison with one source.
- Do not invent a baseline, experiment, consensus, or reported metric from a
  proposal or task fixture.
- Distinguish reported observations, proposed evaluations, and reviewer
  inferences; keep limitations visible without repeating them in every section.
- The review should answer the user's question concisely, not fill space to
  resemble a multi-paper survey.
- Flag unsupported causal, comparative, or generalization claims and suggest
  a concrete bounded correction.
- Do not include pipeline logs, local artifact paths, prompt instructions, or
  an uncited literature-wide claim in the report body.

## Output Expectations

Return structured findings by severity and suggested action. Do not rewrite
the section directly unless the coordinator asks for a revision.
