# Experiment Analysis Report

## Intended Use

A concise record of an unmet or uncertain research goal, not a successful
research paper. Explain the original goal without moving its success criteria.
Use the delivery decision in the evidence, including the stop reason and
continuation options. Do not invent novelty, missing experiments or significance.

## Writing Workflow

Draft order: Task And Conditions -> Observed Results -> Interpretation And Limits -> Next Steps

## Task And Conditions

State the user's goal, tested changes, actual data, protocol and constraints.
Distinguish executed conditions from proposed work.

## Observed Results

Report available measurements, baseline comparability and failures. Preserve
negative results and uncertainties. Missing measurements are not zeros.

## Interpretation And Limits

Explain what was learned, whether the goal was met, why work stopped and what
remains unknown. A successful process exit is not scientific success.

## Next Steps

Summarize the recorded continuation options and evidence needed for another
attempt. Separate technical repairs from research revisions. Do not assert
that suggested work has already run or that more resources are authorized.

## References

References are generated from body citations. Cite literature only for claims
it supports, not as proof of local settings or measurements.
